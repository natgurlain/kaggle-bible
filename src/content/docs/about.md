---
title: About Kaggle Bible
description: The editorial principles and scope of the Kaggle Bible project.
---

Kaggle Bible is a community-maintained reference for learning from past competitions. Its useful unit is an actionable lesson with traceable evidence: what worked, under which conditions, how it was validated, what it cost, and what remains unknown.

The project aims for broad competition coverage over time while keeping every entry honest about its depth. A catalog row is useful for discovery; a guide earns its detail through sources, verification, and review.

Visit the live site at [kaggle-bible.vercel.app](https://kaggle-bible.vercel.app/).

The repository contains the content model, editorial workflow, competition inventory, templates, and website source. See the [project proposal](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/blob/main/docs/proposal.md) for the longer rationale.
