## Reviewed guide

A Level 2 evidence map has inspected primary sources, bounded claims, validation details or explicit gaps, and an independent review. A Level 3 full guide has the additional comparative and practice requirements. Neither label implies execution.

## Executed learning exercise

An original exercise has a downloadable script, a pinned recorded environment, a fixed split and metric, a baseline, one controlled change, and a real run receipt. The receipt records data and script fingerprints, per-split results, seed, elapsed time and the scope of memory measurement. Teaching fixtures, open teaching datasets and authorized competition data are labeled separately.

## Historical solution reproduction

Reproduction means running a specific original solution and testing a specific claim with the documented data, code, environment and tolerance. A successful teaching exercise does not upgrade a historical solution. See the guide's reproduction state and source limitations.

## What we still need to learn

Local checks show that navigation and exercises work under recorded conditions. They do not show that readers learn, complete projects or prefer this format. Voluntary completion reports can identify problems; they are self-reports, not an independent learning study.
