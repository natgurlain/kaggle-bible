A precise correction or one well-located source is enough to help. Choose a form; GitHub shows its fields before you submit. Contributions receive the same evidence review as existing guides.

## Pick one finishable task

Every task below has an **unassigned contributor owner**. Claim a bounded task in its linked issue before starting; a claim does not assign an independent reviewer or authorize data access. Source gaps may finish with a documented negative result. Keep existing corrections and failed attempts in the issue history.

### House Prices: explain an unseen-neighborhood prediction

**Gap:** a fold metric alone does not explain what the neighborhood rule does when a held-out category has no training examples.

**Done:** inspect the [published fixture](/exercises/#exercise-house-neighborhood), its downloadable script and recorded input. Trace one unseen category through the fallback and compare it to the global log-price mean on the same fold. Report the fold and public fixture row locator, the rule used and one limit of random-fold validation. If no such row occurs, document that finding rather than inventing an example.

**Artifact:** a [focused correction or explanation](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/issues/new?template=correction.yml&title=House%20Prices%20unseen-neighborhood%20diagnostic), linked to [#70](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/issues/70). **Owner:** unassigned. **Reviewer needed:** someone who can trace training-only statistics, fallback behavior and log-RMSE scope. This task uses generated homes; it does not execute Ames data.

These tasks extend the [research queue](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/blob/main/docs/research.md#ordered-queue); they do not assign actual-data port slots. At most three ports may be active, with ownership and access recorded in the project issue. Use [#77](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/issues/77) to report a stale task or missing artifact. Start with the [guides](/competitions/) or [learning exercises](/exercises/).

## What to include

State the competition or exercise, one claim, the original source URL and an exact locator. Separate source-reported results, your executed results and editorial interpretation. Include split, metric and relevant conditions; leave missing details unknown. For a run, identify the script and input hashes, environment, command, fold results and resource-measurement scope.

Share public evidence only. Keep credentials, personal information and private or restricted competition data out of issues. A voluntarily reported execution does not establish reader comprehension or reproduce a historical solution.

For a complete guide contribution, use the [content templates](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/tree/main/templates) and [editorial workflow](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/blob/main/docs/editorial-workflow.md).
