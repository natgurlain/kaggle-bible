## What “complete” means

Every competition starts as a **Level 1 catalog record**. It becomes a **Level 2 evidence map** when the problem, metric, validation ideas, and sourced solution findings are mapped. A **Level 3 full guide** adds a reviewed comparison of approaches, reproducibility details, transferable lessons, and documented limitations.

The site keeps source-reported results, reproduced results, and editorial interpretation visibly separate. Missing details stay marked as unknown.

## Start with a path

The [beginner-to-advanced path](/path/) suggests competitions that teach one new decision at a time. The [best practices](/practices/) section connects general advice to evidence from specific competitions, including when a technique does not transfer.
