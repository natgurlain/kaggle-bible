title: Contribute
description: How to add sourced competition findings and improve Kaggle Bible.
---

The easiest contribution is a precise source or a documented correction. Open an issue or pull request in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible) with the competition slug, the claim, the source URL, and what you verified.

## Add a competition finding

1. Start from the templates in [`templates/`](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/tree/main/templates).
2. Capture the exact source, author, publication date when available, and the part of the solution it supports.
3. Label the result as source-reported, reproduced, or editorial interpretation.
4. Record unknowns explicitly; do not fill gaps with plausible guesses.
5. Explain how the finding transfers, and where it may fail.

## Review checklist

- Can a reader identify the competition, task, target, metric, and split?
- Does each important claim have a source or a reproduction note?
- Are public leaderboard results separated from local validation results?
- Are compute, data, and code requirements clear enough to judge reproducibility?
- Does the summary distinguish a competition-specific trick from a reusable practice?

The [editorial workflow](https://github.com/natgurlain/kaggle-bible/blob/main/docs/editorial-workflow.md) defines the full review and update process.
